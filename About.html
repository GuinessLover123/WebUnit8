Welcome to Retro Fix

Retrofix began as a small passion-driven operation focused on repairing and restoring classic and modern hardware that others often overlooked. By taking on difficult repairs and prioritizing quality craftsmanship, the company quickly built a strong reputation for reliability and attention to detail. As demand grew, Retrofix expanded its team and capabilities while staying true to its hands-on roots. Today, it remains committed to preserving gear, extending its lifespan, and delivering trusted, high-quality repair work.
